Who We Are

About Us

Puchols Water Technologies is a trusted strategic provider of engineered equipment, products, and specialized technical services, delivering comprehensive, innovative, sustainable, and cost-effective solutions for water and wastewater processes that support industrial, municipal, and commercial applications across the Caribbean and Latin America.

Our approach is founded on close technical collaboration with engineering firms, utilities, suppliers, and contractors, ensuring proper equipment selection, seamless system integration, and efficient project execution at every stage — from design and engineering through commissioning and ongoing operational support.

We are committed to delivering reliable, efficient, and sustainable equipment, products, and technical services fully aligned with the highest industry standards and the most demanding operational requirements, ensuring our clients receive exceptional quality, performance, and long-term operational support.
