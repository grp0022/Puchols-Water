About Us

Puchols Water Technologies was established in Puerto Rico in 2015. We distinguish ourselves by offering a comprehensive range of engineering equipment, specialized products, technical services, and advisory services for potable water, wastewater, and industrial fluid processes and applications across the municipal, industrial, and commercial markets throughout the Caribbean and Latin America.

With more than 25 years of industry experience, we provide integrated solutions and innovative, reliable, and cost-effective technologies, supported by a highly skilled technical team committed to excellence.

Through strategic partnerships with globally recognized manufacturers and brands, we support engineering firms, utilities, industries, and contractors in the proper specification of technologies, the selection of the most suitable equipment, and access to high-performance solutions at competitive prices.

We provide advisory services and support throughout every stage of a project, from planning and conceptual engineering to installation, commissioning, start-up, and ongoing operational support. In doing so, we deliver reliable results that meet the most demanding international standards for quality, efficiency, and performance.

At Puchols Water Technologies, we strive to be the trusted strategic partner of choice for our clients, contributing experience, technical expertise, and value-driven solutions that support the sustainable success of every project.
